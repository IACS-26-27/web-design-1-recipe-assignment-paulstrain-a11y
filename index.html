Strawberry Cheesecake

Mix crushed graham crackers with melted butter and sugar

First step: Smash 1 1/2 cup graham crackers

Now preheat oven to 325degreesF and mix crackers with 1/2 cup butter melted and 1/4 cup sugar

Now bake for 10 minutes and set aside to cool

For the filling:

Beat cream cheese with added sugar and eggs

In a big mixing bowl, beat cream cheese until smooth

Add sugar and mix

Crack and add 3 large eggs, then mix

Stir in 1 teaspoon vanilla extract and 1/2 cup sour cream

For Topping:

Toss in strawberries, lemon juice, water, sugar and cornstarch

Cook over medium heat for 5 minutes

Assemble:

Pour batter over crust

Bake for 1 hour

Let it sit for 1 hour

Let it chill for at least 4 hours

Pour strawberry topping over it

Ingredients:

1 1/2 cups graham crackers

1/4 cup sugar

1/2 cxup butter

3 large eggs

1 teaspoon vanilla extract

3 blockzs cream cheese

1/2 cup sour cream

2 cups srawberries
